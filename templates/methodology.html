How recommendations work

Transparent, transcript-grounded guidance.

This application is inspired by Common Sense Media’s public methodology but is not an official Common Sense Media product or rating.

Base analysis

A local language model evaluates movie, television, and standalone transcripts with optional attributed summaries. It assigns a youngest recommended age, nine category scores, detailed content advisories, age-band guidance, and exact supporting excerpts.

Personalized matching

The original recommended age is never rewritten. Your comfort settings produce an additional label: Good match, Worth it, Review first, or Poor match. Small mismatches accumulate weighted penalties. “Avoid entirely” and “Very uncomfortable” settings impose hard limits.

Positive content

Positive messages, role models, educational value, and diverse representation act only as tie-breakers. Strong positive content may promote a borderline Review first result to Worth it, but it cannot override a Poor match or hard limit.

Limitations

Subtitles cannot fully describe visual violence, nudity, gestures, product placement, or visual stereotypes. Evidence is checked against transcript text, while external summaries are retained with source provenance.
